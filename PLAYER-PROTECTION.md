# PLAYER PROTECTION — KEEP YOUR ACCOUNT YOURS

**Elarumi | Player Security & Awareness**

Gaming should be enjoyable. No tournament, free reward, exclusive offer or invitation is worth losing access to your account.

This guide brings together official Epic Games security guidance, general online safety recommendations and personal advice from Elarumi.

---

## 1. EPIC GAMES — OFFICIAL ACCOUNT SECURITY GUIDANCE

The following recommendations are based on Epic Games' published account security guidance. Always consult Epic's official support pages for the latest instructions.

### Protect your Epic Games account

- **Use a unique password.** Do not reuse your Epic Games password on other websites or services.
- **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).** Epic supports authenticator apps, email and SMS verification. Choose an available method and keep your recovery options secure.
- **Secure your email account.** Use a unique password and enable 2FA on the email account associated with Epic.
- **Verify your email address.** Maintain access to the email account associated with your Epic account.
- **Protect linked accounts.** Secure linked PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Google and other accounts with unique passwords and 2FA where available.
- **Keep your devices updated.** Update your operating system, browser and security software.
- **Avoid shared-device risks.** Do not save your login details on public or shared devices. Sign out after use.
- **Do not buy, sell or share accounts.** Follow Epic's account rules.
- **Be cautious of unofficial offers.** Do not enter your Epic credentials on third-party websites offering free V-Bucks, games, rewards or tournament access.

## 2. GENERAL PLAYER SAFETY — ADVICE FOR EVERY GAMER

### Before joining a tournament

- Check who is organising the tournament.
- Verify the event through the organiser's established and independently verified channels.
- Be suspicious of unexpected invitations, urgent registration demands and unusually generous rewards.
- Do not assume a tournament is legitimate because someone you know shared it.
- Check the actual website domain, not just its logo, branding or page design.
- Be especially cautious of unfamiliar domains pretending to represent established gaming organisations.

### Before clicking a link

- Inspect the full destination domain before opening it.
- Watch for misspellings, unusual domain endings and misleading subdomains.
- Do not trust shortened links from unknown senders.
- Do not sign in simply because a page displays an Epic Games logo.
- Navigate to official services independently rather than following unexpected login links.

HTTPS and a padlock indicate an encrypted connection; they do not prove that a website is legitimate.

### Before downloading anything

- Avoid unofficial tournament launchers, game clients, cheats and suspicious attachments.
- Download software from trusted official sources.
- Do not disable antivirus or device security because a website tells you to.
- Never run an unknown file just because someone claims it is required to join a competition.

### Protect your personal information

- Never share passwords, one-time verification codes or recovery codes.
- Do not send screenshots containing private account information.
- Be careful about sharing your email address, phone number or account recovery details.
- Do not let someone remotely control your device unless you have independently verified that the support interaction is legitimate.

### If you think your account has been compromised

1. Secure your email account first, if you still control it.
2. Change any reused passwords on affected services.
3. Use the official Epic Games recovery process.
4. Review your account security settings and linked accounts.
5. Enable or restore 2FA.
6. Contact official Epic Games Player Support if you cannot recover access.
7. Preserve suspicious messages and evidence without publicly exposing private information.
8. Warn friends if your account may have been used to send suspicious links.

Do not attempt to confront suspected attackers or access systems without authorisation.

## 3. A PERSONAL MESSAGE FROM ELARUMI

**I want players to think before they click.**

I believe gaming should be about competition, creativity, community and having fun — not worrying about whether the next tournament invitation is an attempt to take your account.

My advice is simple:

- Never let excitement override common sense.
- Never sacrifice your account security for a tournament invitation.
- Never trust a website just because it looks professional.
- Never give someone your password or verification code.
- Take a moment to verify an offer before you act.
- If something feels wrong, walk away and check it independently.

You do not have to join every tournament. You do not have to claim every reward. You do not have to follow every link someone sends you.

**Your account, your progress, your purchases and your time matter. Protect them.**

I would rather see a player miss a suspicious tournament than lose an account they have spent years building.

If you encounter something suspicious, report it through the appropriate official channels. Do not retaliate, harass anyone or put yourself at risk trying to investigate it.

This investigation exists to raise awareness, document concerns and encourage responsible reporting. It is not a reason to assume every tournament or organiser is malicious.

**Stay alert. Protect your account. Look out for other players.**

— **Elarumi**
