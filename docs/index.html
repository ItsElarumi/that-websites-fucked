VIEW EVIDENCE REGISTER — E-016

01 / THE INVESTIGATION

Players deserve better.

A Fortnite-themed tournament invitation promoted an event called "FNCS PRO-LEAGUE", encouraged someone to join a team called "Falcons", and directed them towards a suspicious website.

That combination raised concerns about possible phishing and brand impersonation. This project documents the observations, preserves the available technical findings and records the reporting efforts.

We are independent investigators. We do not own, operate, develop or represent the website under investigation.

02 / TECHNICAL FINDINGS

Follow the evidence.

Investigator-supplied captures of a public Django debug response exposed application and configuration details that warrant security review.

01

Debug information

The supplied capture showed Django 5.1.7, Python 3.12.3 and DEBUG enabled, alongside application implementation details.

02

Configuration exposure

The capture included permissive host and CORS settings and other configuration details. Their effective impact has not been independently tested.

03

Independent research

A separate investigation documented a Fortnite-related phishing kit with several technical similarities. A shared operator or deployment has not been established.

These observations do not prove credential theft, database access, successful exploitation or the identity of any operator. No credentials were submitted and no unauthorised access was attempted.

04 / PLAYER PROTECTION

Protect your account.
Protect your team.

Stop before you sign in.

Be cautious of tournament links sent through unsolicited messages. A professional-looking page does not prove legitimacy.

Verify independently.

Check competitions through official channels. Do not trust a link just because a friend or teammate shared it.

Secure your accounts.

Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication. Never share verification codes or recovery information.

Report suspicious activity.

Preserve relevant messages and report suspicious invitations through the appropriate platform channels.
