Summary of observed activity

A Discord message promoted an alleged Fortnite-related tournament described as "FNCS PRO-LEAGUE" and invited recipients to join a team identified as "Falcons".

The message directed users towards the reported domain, which presented Fortnite-related tournament content and included a dedicated login page.

The combination of gaming-related tournament promotion, apparent Fortnite branding and a login page raised concerns about possible brand impersonation, misleading tournament registration and potential credential harvesting.

Evidence and investigative limitations

- A screenshot of the Discord promotional message has been preserved.
- The reported tournament and login URLs have been recorded.
- Passive RDAP research identified REG.RU LLC as the domain registrar.
- No credentials were submitted.
- No intrusive testing or unauthorised access was performed.
- The identity of the website operator has not been established.
- Official authorisation by Epic Games has not been established.
- Credential collection or theft has not been technically confirmed.

Registrar action

The matter was reported to REG.RU under support reference "20261123373000095".

REG.RU confirmed in writing that the domain had been blocked. Its subsequent clarification stated:

"The domain is currently blocked. Detailed information regarding the block and the reasons for it can only be provided to the domain administrator."

The precise blocking mechanism, duration and reason have not been disclosed.
